# Privacy Policy for PaintQuoteX Interior

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

At EasyQuoteXAu Interior, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data associated with your use of our application.

## Information Collection
Our app is designed to help you calculate and estimate interior painting quotes. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, tracking data, or user credentials on external servers. All calculations and data remain locally on your device.

## In-App Purchases
If you purchase our subscription (`EasyQuote Pro`), the payment processing and data management are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your credit card details or financial information.
